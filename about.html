About School

PM SHRI S S +2 High School, Raidih is committed to quality education, discipline, innovation and holistic development. The school provides an inclusive learning environment with experienced teachers, laboratories, library, sports facilities and co-curricular activities.

Our aim is to develop students academically, socially and morally, preparing them to become responsible citizens and future leaders.

Vision

To empower students with knowledge, values and life skills for a successful future.

Mission

To provide quality education through innovative teaching, discipline and equal opportunities for every learner.
